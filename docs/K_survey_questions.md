# HeatShield pilot survey: 12 questions

## Use and safeguards

This formative questionnaire is for a proposed pilot with **20–50 adults
(18+)** in delivery, construction/outdoor-site work, street vending, and
campus staff with outdoor duties around DTU/Rohini. It is not representative
research, a medical screen, or a measure of illness prevalence. See the
[pilot plan](./K_pilot_plan.md) and [blank consent template](./K_consent_form.md).

No responses are in this document. Do not collect a respondent's name,
phone/email, employer, exact address/GPS, date of birth, medical history,
photograph, or audio/video. Don't collect exact shift schedules if they could
identify a person. Ensure the Google Form does not record emails or require
sign-in, keep the linked response sheet private, and test the actual settings
and branching before distribution. The product should disclose that Google
may process technical metadata under its own terms; do not promise absolute
anonymity.

### Form setup

- **Form title:** HeatShield: heat-safety prototype feedback (DTU/Rohini)
- **Description:** paste the participant information and voluntary
  participation statement from [K_consent_form.md](./K_consent_form.md),
  after filling the approved contact, controller, retention/deletion, and
  withdrawal details. Those placeholders must not remain at launch.
- **Eligibility gate (not counted as a survey question):** “Are you 18 years
  of age or older?” Required, multiple choice: “Yes” / “No”. Route “No” to a
  polite end page; route “Yes” to consent. Do not expose survey items to
  ineligible people.
- **Consent (not counted as a survey question):** after presenting the
  information, use a required multiple-choice item: “I agree to take part” /
  “I do not agree to take part.” Route agreement to the survey and refusal to
  an end page. Do not force consent.
- Use one question per item except where clearly marked checkbox. Keep
  question order fixed. Do not enable email collection or sign-in. Restrict
  response-sheet access to approved team members. Do not link the anonymous
  survey to a separately collected contact list.
- Keep any completed responses, form response links, and contact information
  in approved private storage, never in the repository. Public reporting is
  aggregate only; suppress groups under five.
- Estimated completion time: **8–10 minutes**, to be checked with a small
  usability test before recruitment. Provide a verbally administered option
  only if the participant requests it and agrees; the researcher should not
  infer answers.

## Google Form build and preflight checklist

- [ ] Paste and approve the participant information and privacy/retention
  wording; replace all bracketed fields in the consent template before
  creating a live form.
- [ ] Configure the age gate and consent section; test “under 18” and
  “decline” end paths in respondent preview and confirm neither exposes the
  research items.
- [ ] Configure checkbox response validation for the stated maximum selections
  in questions 5 and 11; verify participants can leave each item unanswered.
- [ ] Check that “collect email addresses” and sign-in restrictions are off;
  test in a logged-out/incognito respondent view. Do not enable one-response
  restrictions if they require sign-in.
- [ ] Link responses only to an approved team-owned sheet; restrict access
  and verify no public link sharing or automatic email collection.
- [ ] Confirm no item requests contact details, employer/site identifiers,
  exact locations, health information, or media uploads.
- [ ] Have a Hindi-first speaker review any translation and test mobile and
  verbal administration; this document currently provides English source
  wording and does not claim translation review.
- [ ] Pilot the questionnaire for comprehension and actual completion time;
  adjust before recruiting. Keep pilot responses private and separate from
  any later study.
- [ ] Do not publish or distribute until site/partner permission and any
  applicable institutional review are confirmed. The first-batch invite is
  only a draft in [K_pilot_plan.md](./K_pilot_plan.md).
