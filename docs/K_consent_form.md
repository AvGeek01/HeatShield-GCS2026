### Why are we inviting you?

We are testing an early prototype that aims to explain heat conditions and
show possible protective actions and city-planning information. We want to
learn whether the questions and prototype are understandable and practical.
This is not medical research or medical advice, does not diagnose illness,
and does not promise that the prototype will keep anyone safe.

### What will happen if I take part?

If you agree, you may answer a short survey of about 8–10 minutes and may
choose to view or discuss an illustrative prototype. Participation is
voluntary. You may skip any question, pause, or stop without giving a reason.
We do not plan to audio/video record or photograph participants.

### What information will be collected?

The survey asks broad work/activity categories and opinions about heat
information and access to practical options. It is designed not to ask for
your name, phone number, email, employer, exact location, or medical history.
Do not type identifying details into a response. The form provider may
process technical metadata under its own terms; therefore the team cannot
promise absolute anonymity.

### Possible discomforts, benefits, and choice

Some questions about hot working conditions may be uncomfortable. You may
skip them or stop. There may be no direct benefit to you. Your choice will
not affect your employment, pay, campus access, public services, or
relationship with any partner. The team will not give a personal safety
diagnosis or replace official heat warnings or workplace protections.

### How will responses be handled?

Responses will be restricted to the authorized study team and reported as
aggregate findings. Raw responses will be retained until **[date above]** and
then deleted under the approved plan. Small groups will not be reported where
people might be identifiable. Any separate request for an interview quote,
photo, audio/video recording, or public identification requires a different
choice and a separate consent process; none is included in this survey
consent.

You may request withdrawal until **[state a practical withdrawal deadline
before use]** by using **[approved contact method]** and giving a response
code only if one is issued. Do not provide your name or contact details in
the survey response.
